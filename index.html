About

Money has always been the thing Tanishq wants to understand properly — not just how to make it, but how it moves, gets priced, gets misread, and gets modelled. That pull toward economics is what took him from a Bachelor of Commerce at Suresh Gyan Vihar University into equity research, and it's what keeps him working through the ACCA syllabus one paper at a time.

"I'm interested in the discipline of getting a number right — and being honest about the assumptions that got you there."

Before finance took over, he spent a year writing — translating dense mental-health research into articles people actually wanted to read. That habit of turning complexity into something clear is still the throughline: whether it's a DCF model or a research note, the job is to make the numbers make sense to someone else.
