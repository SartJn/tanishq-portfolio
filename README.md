- **Investment & Research Case Studies**: Deep dives into:
  - *BKG Securities*: 15+ Equity Portfolio Valuation (+12% Alpha)
  - *Clarity*: Quantitative Research Synthesis (+40% Reader Engagement)
  - *Smart India Hackathon*: Resource & Budget Optimization (-15% Cost Reduction)
- **Dual Theme System**: Seamless toggle between **Archival Ledger** (parchment editorial) and **Bloomberg Terminal Dark** modes.
- **Direct Resume & Verification**: One-click download of the complete [Resume PDF (Tanishq_Finance_ACCA.pdf)](Tanishq_Finance_ACCA.pdf) and interactive credential verification modals.
